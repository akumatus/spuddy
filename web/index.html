How it works

Three little moments

1

Pull a card

Tap the card in his hands and a fresh line flips up. Pull as many as you like — the good ones don't run out.

2

Keep the keepers

Tap Keep it ♥ and the line lands in your Card Book — a little shoebox of good words, saved for the days that need them.

3

Go for gold

Every so often he goes quiet, takes his time, and knits you a golden card by hand. Rare on purpose, kept forever.

✦Rare · Golden Stitch

Sometimes a card is just for you

Most golden cards are a famous line your buddy has been saving up for you, signed by whoever said it first. But once they know you well enough, now and then they weave one from scratch — out of the things you've actually told them — and sign it themselves.

Keep a few, and Prof, the Tenured Tuber will come knocking.

✦ ✦
Say what's on your mind

She listens — and she remembers

Hover over whoever's on duty and a little chat box slides out — today that's Bloom, the Quiet Gardener. Tell her the day was a lot and she'll meet it with soil and rain, a warm line or two, and one easy handle to pick back up. What matters finds its way into your golden cards; everything she remembers lands in one little quilt, and every patch has an ×.

She's not a therapist — if you're really struggling, she'll gently point you toward a human you trust.

What it does

Small, alive, and entirely his own thing

He doesn't take up a window. He doesn't ping you. He just sits in the corner of your day, glancing over when you move your mouse, holding out something kind.

Poke him

Tap for a reaction — squish, hop, wobble, a sudden sneeze — and every so often a whole little skit. Never the same one twice in a row.

He lives his own life

Bored, he entertains himself. Sleepy, he dozes off. Missing you, he taps the glass to say hi. Sit too long and he'll suggest a stretch; stay up too late and he'll nudge you toward bed.

He watches your cursor

Eyes lead, the head follows — across the whole screen. He blinks, glances around, looks right at you… then drifts back to daydreaming when you wander off.

Drag him anywhere

Fling him sideways and he spins with real momentum, then springs upright. Nudge him to a screen edge and he curls up for a nap.

Meet the buddies

Six friends, each a different kind of care

Spud starts with you. The other five are earned, never bought — keep · favorite · confide · show up · go gold. Once a friend, always a friend.
